Install SlyRun on iOS

1
Open SlyRun in Safari (not Chrome, not WhatsApp's in-app browser).
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add in the top right. SlyRun now appears on your home screen like a real app.
Note: iOS requires the app to be opened from a real URL (not a local file). If your home-screen icon opens with a URL bar, use the hosting guide in the README.
